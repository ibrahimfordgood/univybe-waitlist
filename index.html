Questions

You asked. VYBE answered.

What is UniVybe, exactly?

A learning platform that turns your courses into short, interactive lessons with quizzes, XP, streaks, leaderboards and a student feed. It starts with learning and grows into portfolios, credentials and real opportunities.

Is it free to join the waitlist?

Yes. Joining the waitlist is free and you can leave any time. We'll share details on plans and pricing before launch.

When does it launch?

We're launching campus by campus, starting in Sierra Leone. Waitlist members hear first, and campuses with the most sign-ups unlock first.

I'm a lecturer or run an institution. Is this for me?

Yes. Pick "Educator" or "Institution" on the form. UniVybe gives educators better content tools and engagement insights, and helps institutions bring structured, modern learning online.

Why a lightbulb?

Because every "ohhh, I get it now" moment deserves a glow-up. VYBE glows brighter when you learn, and dims when you've been procrastinating. 👀
